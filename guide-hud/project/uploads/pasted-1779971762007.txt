The colonial fleets promised a thousand bright worlds. They built them, switched off the lights, and left. What grew back in the dark wasn't utopia — it was companies.
You worked for one of them. United Stellar Armament holds your bond now: a number that climbs while you sleep, secured against a body you'd quite like to keep. There's no honest work on this rim that pays it down, and you've stopped looking for any.
So you're chasing a rumor instead. Out past the wastes stands a megamall — a cathedral raised for a civilization that loved buying things and forgot how to defend them. Everything valuable that ever went inside is still in there. So is whatever killed the last people who went looking for it.
You have a weapon, a debt, and a bad idea. Out here, that's a beginning.
